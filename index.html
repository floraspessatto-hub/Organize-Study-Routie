Subject Progress Breakdown

Feynman Technique

Explain a concept in simple words as if teaching it to a 10-year-old child. Whenever you encounter a gap or difficulty in explanation, revisit your notes to master that specific concept.

Spaced Repetition

Review newly learned material at increasing intervals (e.g., 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days later). This moves information from short-term to long-term memory.

Pomodoro Technique

Work with complete focus for 25 minutes, then take a short 5-minute break. After 4 cycles, take a longer 15-30 minute break to maintain peak focus and energy.

Active Recall

Instead of passively re-reading notes or highlighting textbooks, test your memory by answering flashcards, writing summary sheets, or taking practice quizzes without looking.
